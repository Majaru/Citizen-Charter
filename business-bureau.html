Main Services

Track Your Application

The full Citizen's Charter lineup of 12 official services — tap any card for complete requirements and the step-by-step process.

Service 1 of 12

Issuance of Business Permit

Processing and release of official permits allowing a new business to legally operate within the city.

Service 2 of 12

Renewal of Business Permit

Annual renewal of an existing business permit to continue lawful operation into the next calendar year.

Service 3 of 12

Occupational Permit

Processing of permits for employees and workers as proof of compliance with health, safety, and city requirements.

Service 4 of 12

Special Permit

Issuance of temporary or special authorization for specific business activities, events, or operations requiring additional approval.

Service 5 of 12

Issuance of Certification

Preparation and release of official certifications related to business records, permits, and compliance documents.

Service 6 of 12

Amendment of Business Permit

Processing of changes to an active permit — address, business line, ownership, name, or other corrections.

Service 7 of 12

Cancellation of Business Permit

Termination or revocation of a business permit due to request, non-operation, or non-compliance with regulations.

Service 8 of 12

Partial Retirement of Business Permit

Removal of a specific business line, branch, or activity from a permit while the rest of the business continues operating.

Service 9 of 12

Full Retirement of Business Permit

Processing the permanent closure or discontinuation of a business and cancellation of its active permit records.

Service 10 of 12

Ocular Inspection

On-site inspection of business establishments to verify compliance with safety, sanitation, zoning, and operational requirements.

Service 11 of 12

ICAB (Internet Café Accreditation)

Accreditation required for Internet Café and Coin-Operated Internet Machine (Pisonet) operators, prior to Business Permit issuance.

Service 12 of 12

Business Complaint

Filing of a complaint against a business establishment whose activities threaten public welfare, security, or safety.
