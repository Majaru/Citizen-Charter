Download and print your Unified Application Form, then submit it along with your other requirements to the Business Bureau Office at the Ground Floor, Sangguniang Panlungsod Building, Davao City.

View Requirements Checklist
  1. Duly filled-out Unified Application Form (1 original)
  2. Sketch / Google map of business location (1 original) with pictures of the premise location
  3. Certified List of Employees / Certification of No Employee (1 original, notarized)
  4. Valid DTI Registration for Single Proprietorship (1 photocopy)
  5. SEC Registration, Articles of Incorporation for Partnership and Corporation (1 photocopy)
  6. Certificate of Cooperation, Articles of Cooperation for Cooperative (1 photocopy)
  7. DOLE / POEA Certificate of Registration, if applicable
  8. DHSUD Certificate of Registration, if applicable
  9. Special Power of Attorney (1 original) for represented Sole Proprietorship
  10. Partnership Resolution (1 original) for represented Partnership
  11. Secretary's Certificate or Board Resolution (1 original) for Corporation and Cooperative
  12. Valid ID of representatives, owners, and Corporate Secretary (1 photocopy each)
  13. Contract of Lease and proof of ownership by the Lessor, if rented (1 photocopy)
  14. Proof of ownership, if business location is owned
  15. Consent and proof of ownership, if location is freely used (1 photocopy)
  16. OR/CR of vehicle, if with delivery vehicle (1 photocopy each)
  17. Consent for use of vehicle, if not owned by the applicant
  18. Valid Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) for Business
  19. Other requirements depending on the business activity declared

Note: requirements may vary depending on business structure and line of business. Please confirm with the Business Bureau for your specific case.
