About the Business Bureau

Who We Are

The Business Bureau standardizes all business establishments operating in Davao City and provides the transacting public with trouble-free transactions and prompt services. It has adopted technology enhancements and local interconnection among regulatory offices to ensure fast and reliable services to its clients.

In 2004, a business one-stop shop was established, and during every renewal period, a satellite office is implemented to provide an additional venue for transacting clients. The office was established to issue permits and licenses through efficient and quality service to the public, and through impartial and uniform enforcement of the law — providing an effective system and practices in issuing business permits and licenses to the entrepreneurs of Davao City.

City Mayor's Office - Business Bureau

Mandate

To provide efficient delivery of services to the public by reducing bureaucratic red tape, preventing graft and corruption, and providing penalties therefor.

Mission

To issue permits and licenses through efficient and quality service, fostering a sense of impartiality and uniform enforcement of the law.

Vision

A highly systematic and business-friendly organization providing excellent public service in the issuance of permits and licenses.

Organizational Outcome

Excellent and quality service, intensified revenue collection, and a strengthened monitoring system.

Organizational Chart

The Business Bureau operates under the City Mayor's Office – Permits & Licenses Division, staffed by a dedicated team of licensing officers and support personnel committed to efficient and impartial public service.
