Citizen's Charter

The Business Bureau, Office of the City Mayor of Davao City, is committed to providing prompt, efficient, and courteous services to all clients in accordance with Republic Act 11032 (Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018). All services listed below are External Services under the City Mayor's Office – Permits & Licenses Division.

Services Overview

  1. Pre-Assessment
  2. Issuance of Business Permit
  3. Special Permit
  4. Occupational Permit
  5. Issuance of Certifications / Certified True Copy
  6. Full Retirement of Business Permit
  7. Cancellation of Business Permit
  8. Ocular Inspection & ICAB Inspection

Issuance of Business Permit

It shall be unlawful for any person or entity to conduct or engage in any business, trade, or occupation within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City without first securing a permit from the City Mayor and paying the necessary fees to the City Treasurer. The Business Permit is valid for not more than one (1) year, expiring on December 31, and must be renewed within January each year.

Occupational Permit

Issued to any worker or employee who is not a holder of a validly issued Professional Tax Receipt, whether working on a temporary or permanent basis. Renewed annually on or before January 31. Late renewal is subject to a 25% penalty.
Office / DivisionCity Mayor's Office – Permits & Licenses Division
ClassificationSimple
Type of TransactionG2C – Government to Citizen
Who May AvailAny employee or worker who is a Filipino citizen, 18 years of age at time of application. Foreigners must present an Alien Employment Permit from DOLE. For workers below 18 years of age, an Affidavit of Consent from a parent or guardian is required.

Cancellation of Business Permit

Offered to any natural or juridical entity whose application for Business Permit has been encoded in the system but the applicant later decided not to push through the said application for any valid reason. Cancellation must be applied within three (3) months from the date of application. Not available to applicants with records at the City Treasurer's Office.
Office / DivisionCity Mayor's Office – Permits & Licenses Division
ClassificationSimple
Type of TransactionG2C – Government to Citizen
Who May AvailAny natural or juridical entity with a valid reason to cancel a duly processed application for new business permit.

5. Partnership Resolution – Partnership (1 original + 1 photocopy)Applicant, notarized

9. ID of authorized representative (1 photocopy)Taxpayer

Ocular Inspection & ICAB Inspection

Business activities being applied for will be validated through ocular inspections. For Internet Cafes, this procedure issues Internet Café Accreditation (ICAB) to internet cafes and coin-operated internet machine business operators who are compliant with internet café regulation requirements. ICAB Accreditation is a specific requirement for the issuance of a Business Permit.

ClassificationSimple (Regular), Complex (Complaint), Highly Technical (ICAB Accreditation)
Type of TransactionG2C – Government to Citizen; G2B – Government to Business
Who May AvailAny business owners operating in Davao City (Regular); any natural or juridical entities whose welfare, security, and safety are threatened by any business activities (Complaint); Internet Café and Coin Operated Internet Machine Business Operators within Davao City (ICAB).
