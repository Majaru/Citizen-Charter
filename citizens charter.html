Citizen's Charter

The Business Bureau, Office of the City Mayor, is committed to providing prompt, efficient, and courteous service to all clients in accordance with Republic Act 11032 (Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018). Below is an overview of our services and the standards we uphold.

Business Permit Services

The Business Bureau is committed to providing efficient, transparent, and professional services for all business permit applications and renewals. Our processes are designed to streamline compliance, reduce processing time, and ensure a fair and impartial licensing experience for every applicant in Davao City.

Business Bureau

Streamlining business registration through reliable, technology-driven systems for an efficient and impartial licensing process.
