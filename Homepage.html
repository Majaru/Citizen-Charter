Citizen's Charter Overview

Business Permit Services:

The Business Bureau is committed to providing efficient, transparent, and professional services for all business permit applications and renewals. Our processes are designed to streamline compliance, reduce processing time, and ensure a fair and impartial licensing experience for every applicant in Davao City.

Main Services

Pre-Assessment

Initial evaluation of business requirements and documents before permit processing to ensure compliance with local regulations.

View Details

Issuance of Business Permit

Processing and release of official permits allowing businesses to legally operate within the city.

View Details

Special Permit

Issuance of temporary or special authorization for specific business activities, events, or operations requiring additional approval.

View Details

Occupational Permit

Processing of permits for employees and workers as proof of compliance with health, safety, and city requirements.

View Details

Issuance of Certifications

Preparation and release of official certifications related to business records, permits, and compliance documents.

View Details

Full Retirement of Business Permit

Processing the permanent closure or discontinuation of a business and cancellation of its active permit records.

View Details

Cancellation of Business Permit

Termination or revocation of a business permit due to request, non-operation, or non-compliance with regulations.

View Details

Ocular Inspection

On-site inspection of business establishments to verify compliance with safety, sanitation, zoning, and operational requirements.
